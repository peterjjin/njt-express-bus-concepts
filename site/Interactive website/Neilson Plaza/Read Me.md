# Neilson Plaza alternative

## Concept 1 · Rutgers → Secaucus Junction

Stadium West Lot → Route 18 → Albany @ George (HELIX access) → Burnet Street on-ramp → Route 18 south → Neilson Plaza → Exit 9 → NJ Turnpike → Exit 15X → Secaucus Junction. Uses Rutgers H/HLX, NJ TRANSIT 818 and 124 geometry where applicable. No intermediate source-route stops are proposed.

## Concept 2 · 8A P&R → Secaucus Junction

South Brunswick 8A P&R → Exit 8A → NJ Turnpike → Exit 15X → Secaucus Junction. Optional diversion exits at 9, calls at Neilson Plaza, and re-enters at 9. This is an off-highway P&R stop, not a stop on the exit ramp.

## Concept 3 · Molly Pitcher P&R via Exit 8

Proposed Molly Pitcher Service Area P&R → assumed rear-gate opening → Half Acre Road → US 130 → Route 133 → Turnpike Exit 8 northbound → Secaucus Junction. Optional pickups: South Brunswick Exit 8A P&R and Neilson Plaza at Exit 9. No direct northbound entry from the southbound service area is assumed.

## Concept 3 access assumption

Molly Pitcher is a southbound service area. This concept assumes authorization to open the gated rear service-road connection to Half Acre Road; it is not existing public access. Buses use local roads and enter the Turnpike northbound at Exit 8. P&R use, coach access, gate/security changes and parking capacity are unapproved.

The optional Exit 8A and Exit 9 pickups can be toggled independently. GeoJSON/CSV variants: core, optional (Exit 9), optional_8a (Exit 8A), optional_both (both pickups). No new connector is certified as an existing public road.

Planning-level, northbound conceptual alignment only; not an operating timetable or approved bus route. GTFS shapes establish mapped alignments, not legal access or continuous current service. OSRM is a passenger-car routing profile, not a coach-clearance validation. Return-direction routing, turns, ramp movements, tolls, curbside access, layover, schedules and operator agreements require review. No travel-time or ridership estimates are asserted. Existing-route coverage is selected, not exhaustive; Warren review pending.
